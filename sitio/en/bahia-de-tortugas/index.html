Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi to Bahía de Tortugas from Campeche?

$4,000 MXN in a private sedan for up to 4 people: ride there, up to 6 hours of waiting and ride back, tolls included.

Why is waiting included?

Because there is no transport back to Campeche from the beach club. We wait for you and bring you back.

What if we want to stay longer?

Each additional 15 minutes after the 6 hours costs $100.

Is there an entrance fee?

No, the club has no entrance fee; you pay for what you eat and drink.
