Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Campeche Airport?

From $300 MXN in a sedan for up to 4 people, depending on your destination. Private van for up to 14 people: $1,500. Shared shuttle: $150 per person.

What if my flight is delayed?

We wait for you. Send us your flight number when booking.

Can I book before I arrive?

Yes. Book online or by WhatsApp with your flight date and time and we will be waiting for you.

Can I pay by card and get an invoice?

Yes, debit or credit card, and electronic invoice (CFDI).

Do you work early in the morning?

Yes, 24 hours, every day. For early flights we recommend booking the day before.
