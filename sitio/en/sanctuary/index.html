Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi to the Uayamón Sanctuary?

From downtown Campeche $450 one way; from the Tren Maya station $600. Package with ride there, up to 2 h 45 min waiting and back: $1,100.

Is there phone signal at the Sanctuary?

Almost none. Sometimes AT&T gets a little. That is why it is best to book your ride back in advance.

What time is mass?

Saturday 6:00 pm and Sunday 11:00 am. See the schedule on this page.

Can I pay by card and get an invoice?

Yes, by card or transfer, but it is paid when you board because there is almost no signal at the sanctuary. We also give invoices.

Why is it more from the Tren Maya?

Because it includes waiting: if your train is up to 20 minutes late, we do not charge waiting time.
