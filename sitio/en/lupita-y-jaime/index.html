Where to eat near Edzná

Lupita and Jaime, home cooking 1 km from Edzná

Store and diner in Uzahzil-Edzná, on the road to Cayal. Breakfast and traditional Campeche food, every dish with beans, rice and salsa.

💬 WhatsApp Lupita and Jaime 📞 Call 981 104 8109 📍 How to get there
📵 There is almost no phone signal thereMessage them on WhatsApp before you leave Campeche so your food is ready. Once there, you can use their wifi.

Always on the menu

🥩 Fried pork
🥩 Beef steak
🥗 Salpicón
🍗 Chicken breast, breaded (milanesa) or grilled
🍳 Breakfast
Every dish comes with beans, rice and salsa. They also have a store with cold drinks.
🕖 Open every day, 7 am to 6 pm
📅 Order aheadBook a day ahead and they cook it for your day: pork in tomato sauce, pork in guajillo chili sauce, adobo chicken with potatoes, chicken escabeche, and puchero vaquero (with chaya, pork ribs and rice). Order on WhatsApp →

Frequently asked questions

Where can I eat near Edzná?

At Lupita and Jaime's, 1 km from the archaeological site on the road to Cayal, in Uzahzil-Edzná. Breakfast and traditional food every day from 7 am to 6 pm.

Can I order food ahead?

Yes. Message +52 981 104 8109 on WhatsApp before you leave (there is almost no signal there) and they will cook dishes to order, like puchero vaquero or chicken escabeche.

How do I get there from Campeche?

By Taxicel taxi: the Edzná trip with waiting is $1,800 MXN and we can stop here to eat.
