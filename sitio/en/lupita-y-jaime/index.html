Frequently asked questions

Where can I eat near Edzná?

At Lupita and Jaime's, 1 km from the archaeological site on the road to Cayal, in Uzahzil-Edzná. Breakfast and traditional food from 7 am to 6 pm.

Can I order food ahead?

Yes. Call or message +52 981 104 8109 in advance and they will cook dishes to order, like puchero vaquero or chicken escabeche.

How do I get there from Campeche?

By Taxicel taxi: the Edzná trip with waiting is $1,800 MXN and we can stop here to eat.
