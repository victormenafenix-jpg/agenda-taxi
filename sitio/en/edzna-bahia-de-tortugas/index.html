Frequently asked questions

How much is the Edzná and Bahía de Tortugas tour from Campeche?

$5,400 MXN in a private sedan for up to 4 people, tolls and water included. With a guide at Edzná: $6,200.

What time do we leave?

At 7:00 am, to reach Edzná when it opens and avoid the heat. We are back in Campeche around 5:30 pm.

Are entrance fees and meals included?

No. Edzná entrance is paid at the ticket office; Bahía de Tortugas has no entrance fee and you pay for what you eat.

Do you pick me up at my hotel?

Yes, at your hotel or anywhere in Campeche.
